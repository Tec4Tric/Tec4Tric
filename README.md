## 👨‍🏫 About
I'm an **Assistant Professor** at [Dr. B. C. Roy Engineering College](https://www.bcrec.ac.in/) and a **Ph.D. Research Scholar at IIIT Kalyani**, based in Kolkata, India.
My work focuses on **knowledge graphs, NLP and question answering for scholarly and agricultural text**.

### 🔬 Research interests
- Knowledge graph construction from scientific literature
- Named entity recognition and relation extraction
- Scholarly and domain-specific question answering
- Temporal and causal reasoning over papers and claims
- LLMs, GraphRAG and retrieval-augmented generation
